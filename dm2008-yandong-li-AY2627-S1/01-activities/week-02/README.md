# Week 02 - Conditions and Loops

I used conditions and loops to play with colour, speed and repeating shapes. Small changes in the rules gave the motion quite a different feel.

| Activity | What I made |
| --- | --- |
| [2a - Mode Switch](activity-a/index.html) | Two moving circles with keyboard and mouse inputs that change their colour and speed. |
| [2b - Pattern Making](activity-b/index.html) | Rows of circles moving in opposite directions, leaving soft trails. |

In 2a, Q/W/E change the background. In 2b, holding the mouse changes the speed, and holding a key varies the grey values.
